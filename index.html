How Tamale Market Finder works for buyers and sellers

1. About Tamale Market Finder

Tamale Market Finder ("TMF", "we", "the platform") is a digital marketplace operated by TechMarketVulture that connects buyers with local traders, service providers, hotels, eateries, and businesses in the Tamale metropolitan area and surrounding communities. TMF lists products and services, enables WhatsApp contact between buyers and sellers, provides directions to physical stall or business locations, and offers optional delivery arrangements between parties.

2. Who Can Use TMF

Anyone in or around Tamale can browse listings and contact sellers for free — no account required. To list products or services, you must register as a trader and provide accurate information about your business. You must be at least 18 years old to create a trader account. By using TMF, you confirm that all information you provide is truthful and current.

3. Buying on TMF

TMF facilitates contact between buyers and sellers but does not process payments, hold inventory, or guarantee delivery. All transactions — including payment, delivery, and returns — are arranged directly between you and the seller, typically through WhatsApp. TMF's "Order Now" feature sends a reservation message to the seller; it is not a guaranteed purchase until the seller confirms.

Delivery options shown on listings are set by individual traders. If a trader offers delivery, the terms (cost, area, timing) are agreed directly between you and the trader. TMF is not responsible for delivery failures or disputes.

The "Take Me There" button opens directions to the seller's registered location via Google Maps. TMF does not guarantee the accuracy of the location pin or that the seller will be available at that location at all times. Always confirm operating hours with the seller before visiting.

We recommend meeting sellers in person at their verified stall or business location for payment and pickup. Always inspect goods before paying.

4. Selling on TMF

As a trader, you are responsible for the accuracy of your listings — including product descriptions, prices, photos, stock availability, and business location. You agree to respond to buyer inquiries promptly and in good faith. Listings that are fraudulent, misleading, or illegal will be removed, and your account may be banned.

You can create listings via the standard onboarding flow or the "Quick List" mode for faster posting. Regardless of the method used, all listings are subject to the same accuracy and quality standards.

TMF offers optional Ghana Card verification to display a verified badge on your listings. Verification increases buyer trust but does not constitute an endorsement by TMF or TechMarketVulture. You are responsible for providing genuine verification documents; submitting falsified documents will result in permanent account ban.

If you enable delivery as a trader, you are solely responsible for fulfilling deliveries you have agreed to. TMF does not employ, vet, or guarantee delivery personnel.

5. Prohibited Listings & Conduct

You may not list or attempt to sell: counterfeit goods, stolen items, illegal drugs, weapons, counterfeit currency, or any item prohibited by Ghanaian law. You may not use TMF to scam, defraud, or harass other users. Prohibited conduct includes:

• Posting fake listings or inflated prices to manipulate the platform
• Creating multiple accounts to bypass restrictions
• Using another trader's photos or business details without permission
• Soliciting payments outside of agreed-upon transaction terms

TMF actively scans listings for suspicious keywords and pricing anomalies using automated detection. Reported listings are reviewed and may be removed without notice. Repeat offenders will be permanently banned.

6. User Safety & Anti-Scam Features

TMF provides several tools to help you make informed decisions:

• Verified seller badges — displayed when a trader completes Ghana Card verification
• Account age display — shows how long a trader has been on the platform
• Price anomaly warnings — flags listings with prices significantly below market average
• Report/flag system — lets any user flag suspicious listings for review

Despite these features, TMF does not guarantee that any seller is trustworthy. Always exercise caution when meeting strangers, sending money, or sharing personal information. Meet in public market areas during daylight hours when possible. Never pay in advance unless you trust the seller.

7. Privacy & Data

TMF collects your name, phone number, WhatsApp number, and business location to create listings and facilitate contact with buyers. Your phone number and WhatsApp number are shared with buyers who contact you through the platform. TMF does not sell your personal data to third parties.

Location data is used only to show directions to your stall or business via the "Take Me There" feature. Your exact GPS coordinates are not displayed to buyers — only approximate directions are provided.

Listing photos you upload are stored on TMF's servers and displayed publicly on the platform. Do not upload images that contain personal information (e.g., ID cards, utility bills) unless explicitly requested for verification.

8. App Installation (PWA)

Tamale Market Finder is a Progressive Web App (PWA) and can be installed on supported mobile devices and browsers. Installing the app adds it to your home screen for quick access but does not grant the platform additional permissions or access to your device. The installed app runs within your browser's security sandbox and follows the same privacy and data policies described above.

Offline access is limited — previously viewed listings may be available from cache, but creating listings, contacting sellers, and loading new content require an internet connection.

9. Liability

TMF is a directory and communication platform operated by TechMarketVulture. We are not a party to any transaction between buyers and sellers. We are not liable for losses, damages, disputes, or fraudulent activity arising from transactions conducted through the platform.

TechMarketVulture and TMF are provided "as is" without warranty of any kind. We do not guarantee uninterrupted access, error-free operation, or the accuracy of listings.

If you experience fraud or a dispute, contact the seller directly first. If the matter cannot be resolved, you may report it to TMF administrators and, if necessary, to the Ghana Police Service.

10. Reporting & Moderation

You can report any listing using the 🚩 flag button on the listing card, or through the report form in Account Settings. Reports are reviewed by TMF administrators within 48 hours where possible. Repeated violations may result in account suspension or permanent ban. TMF reserves the right to remove any listing or account at its sole discretion, without prior notice.

11. Account Termination

You may delete your trader account at any time through Account Settings. Upon deletion, your active listings will be removed, but your data may be retained for a reasonable period for dispute resolution and legal compliance purposes. TMF may terminate or suspend your account if you violate these Terms.

12. Changes to These Terms

TechMarketVulture may update these Terms & Conditions at any time. Significant changes will be highlighted in the app. Continued use of the platform after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated terms.
