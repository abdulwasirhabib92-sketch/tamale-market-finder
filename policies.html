Tamale Market Finder is a listing and discovery platform. We do not handle payment or delivery of any product. Every sale is strictly between the buyer and the seller.

Terms of Service

What TMF is

Tamale Market Finder is a listing and discovery service that connects buyers with independent sellers (Traders) in Tamale and other cities. We publish listings and help buyers find them.

What TMF is not

TMF is not a party to any sale. We do not sell goods, do not take payment for goods, do not hold money in escrow, do not arrange or perform delivery, and do not guarantee quality, safety, legality or availability. Every sale is a direct contract between buyer and Trader. Price, payment, delivery, inspection, returns and refunds are agreed between them.

Your account

You must be 18 or older. One account per person or business. You are responsible for your password and everything done under your account. False identity is grounds for removal.

Buying

Prefer Verified Traders. Verification means we checked identity and business details; it is not a guarantee of any product. Agree price, payment and delivery directly with the Trader before paying. Inspect goods where possible before paying; paying in advance is at your own risk. Report scams, harassment or counterfeit goods immediately.

Selling

List only goods and services you legally own and can supply. Show real photos, real prices, real stock. Honour the price and terms you publish. Respond to buyer enquiries within a reasonable time. We may remove any listing and suspend any account that breaks these rules.

Disputes between users

Disputes about goods, money or delivery are between buyer and Trader. We may share the contact details and listing history needed to help resolve one, but we do not adjudicate, refund or compensate. Report serious fraud and we may remove the account.

Fees

Browsing and buying are free. Sellers pay only for the optional Verified Trader programme. We never charge commission on a sale, because we never handle sale money.

Suspension and removal

We may suspend or remove accounts or listings for breaking these terms, for fraud, for repeated buyer complaints, or where required by law.

Liability

The service is provided as is. To the extent Ghanaian law allows, TMF and TechMarketVulture are not liable for indirect losses, lost profit, or losses arising from a transaction between users. Nothing here limits liability for fraud or for anything that cannot lawfully be excluded.

Changes and governing law

We may update these terms; material changes will be announced in the app. These terms are governed by the laws of the Republic of Ghana.

Privacy Policy

What we collect

Account data: name, email, phone, password stored hashed by our auth provider. Trader data: shop name, market area, Ghana Post GPS address, WhatsApp number, listings, photos. Location: your device GPS position, only if you allow it. Usage: pages viewed, searches, saved shops, orders you record. Payment data for the Verified Trader fee is handled by our payment provider; we never see or store your card or mobile money PIN.

Why we use it

To show nearby shops and rank listings by distance, to let buyers contact sellers, to keep accounts secure, to fix problems, and to meet legal obligations.

Location specifically

Location is requested when the app loads. Allow it and the coordinates are saved on your own device, powering shops near you and map centring. Deny it and the app still works, ranked by other signals. We do not publish your location to other users and we do not track you in the background.

Sharing

Sellers see what you send them when you contact them. Buyers see the contact details a Trader publishes. Payment providers, hosting and database providers process data on our behalf. We disclose data to authorities only where legally required.

Security and retention

Data is transmitted over HTTPS and stored with access controls. Passwords are not stored in readable form. We keep data while the account is active and for as long as needed to meet legal or accounting duties.

Your rights

Under the Ghana Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843) you may ask what we hold about you, request correction, request deletion, or withdraw consent for location. Contact us through Help and Support in the app. You may also complain to the Data Protection Commission of Ghana.

Children

Not for under-18s. If we learn an account belongs to a minor we will close it.

Verified Trader Programme

What it is

An optional paid programme that shows buyers the seller's identity and business have been checked by TMF.

Fees

Verification fee GHS 20, one-off, charged when you apply. Subscription GHS 10 per month, in advance, to keep the badge. Prices are in Ghana cedis and include applicable taxes. Fees are charged via our payment provider by mobile money or card. The verification fee is non-refundable once review has begun.

What verification means

We check your name against a Ghana Card or other government ID, your phone number, your business name and location, and that you can be reached. A verified seller who changes name, phone or business must re-verify.

What verification does not mean

It is not insurance, escrow, a warranty, or a guarantee of quality. TMF does not back any transaction, does not hold money and does not deliver goods.

Renewal and lapse

The badge stays while the subscription is paid. If payment fails we allow a 7 day grace period, then the badge is removed and the listing drops in ranking. Listings stay live but unverified. Re-activating requires payment of the next month, not the missed one. Fees already paid are not refunded for time unused.

Removal

The badge is withdrawn, without refund, for fraud, counterfeit or prohibited goods, repeated buyer complaints, or misuse of the badge.

For buyers

Buy from Verified Traders where you can, and understand plainly that verification is a check of identity, not a guarantee of the goods.

Marketplace Rules

Prohibited items

Weapons, ammunition, explosives; illegal drugs and unlicensed medicines; human remains or body parts; live animals where sale is restricted by law; counterfeit or stolen goods; protected wildlife products; pornographic material; other people's documents or IDs; anything requiring a licence the seller does not hold.

Food, medicine and agriculture

Food must be fit for consumption and honestly described. Agrochemicals must be labelled and legally sold. Veterinary medicines require a licensed seller. Spoiled or expired stock may not be listed.

Listing standards

Real photos of the actual item. State unit, quantity, condition, and whether the price includes delivery. No keyword stuffing, no fake reviews, no impersonation, no links designed to defraud.

Behaviour

No harassment, hate speech, threats, spam, or moving users off platform to defraud them.

Enforcement

First breach: listing removed and a warning. Serious or repeated breaches: suspension or permanent ban, and where money is involved we cooperate with the Ghana Police Service.
